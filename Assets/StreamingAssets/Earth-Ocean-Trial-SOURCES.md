# QA-only July 2004 Earth ocean-response study
Ocean seed source: unmodified NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble: Next Generation (Reto Stockli, NASA GSFC), world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg. See Earth-Base-Trial-SOURCES.md for URL/checksum. Rendering still uses the separately credited 8K relief composite as the land-and-ocean-detail texture.
At 2700x1350 select pixels within 3 RGB codes of the base product's uniform deep-ocean RGB (2,5,20); retain only components reached from three Atlantic/Pacific/Indian blue-water seeds, with longitude wrapping. Erode by eight texels; transition over the next 14. Fade between absolute latitudes 65 and 72 and exclude poles. No pixel in the mask overlaps the checked legacy bright-neutral/ice heuristic; coasts and cold regions are intentionally conservative.
The mask selects where to reduce baked color contrast and apply a modest dielectric roughness shift. It is not a coast survey, ocean depth, live weather, or physical reflectance measurement. NASA does not endorse this game.
